```text
Supermetrics:  30,000 customers, 300 employees, $50M+ revenue, 13 years
```

## The Three Strategies That Actually Work

```text
Strategy 1:  Don't steal customers. Catch NEW ones first.
Strategy 2:  Don't compete on features. Compete on the PROBLEM you solve.

```

## Strategy 1: Catch New Customers Before Supermetrics Does

Every day, new marketers face the data problem for the first time:
- A startup hires their first performance marketer
- A brand starts running TikTok ads alongside Facebook
- An agency signs a new client and needs reporting
- A company enters Southeast Asia and adds new platforms

**These people haven't chosen a tool yet.** They Google "facebook ads to bigquery" or "marketing reporting automation" and pick whatever they find first.

```text
THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY YOU WANT TO OWN:

    Marketer has a new problem
    "I run ads on 3 platforms, how do I see all data together?"
            │
            ▼
    Googles: "cross channel marketing report"
            │
            ▼
    Finds: OrbitX article "How to unify marketing data in 5 minutes"
            │
            ▼
    Clicks: [Use This Template] — one click import
            │
            ▼
    Signs up free → connects 2 sources → sees unified data
            │
            ▼
    "Wow, this is what I needed"
            │
            ▼
    Never evaluates Supermetrics because the problem is already solved
```

**You don't need to beat Supermetrics. You need to get there first.**

How:
- Start writing SEO content NOW (it takes 3-6 months to rank)
- Target long-tail keywords where Supermetrics doesn't rank well
- Every article includes a one-click template
- Make sign-up → working pipeline under 5 minutes

**The math:** If 1,000 new marketers search for "tiktok ads reporting" each month, and OrbitX captures 5% of them, that's 50 new signups/month. If 15% convert to paid, that's 7-8 new customers/month. In 6 months, you have 40-50 customers = 100K+ baht/month.

## The Real Competitive Advantage Is Speed

```text
Supermetrics is a SHIP.
  - 300 people. Every feature takes 6 months.
  - Can't add unified schema (would break existing customers)
  - Can't add AI (not in their DNA/architecture)
  - Can't lower prices (would cannibalize $50M revenue)

OrbitX is a SPEEDBOAT.
  - 1 person. Ship a feature in 1 week.
  - Can design unified schema from day 1 (no legacy)
  - Can build AI-native (architecture supports it)
  - Can price at $79/mo (no revenue to protect)

You don't beat the ship by being a bigger ship.
You beat it by being faster, more nimble, and going
where the ship can't turn.
```

**Supermetrics CANNOT:**
- Add unified schema (would break 30,000 customers' existing pipelines)
- Add AI insights (their architecture is source → destination pipe, no intelligence layer)
- Lower prices (would destroy their revenue)
- Serve Thai market (not worth their time at their scale)
- Build visual workflow builder (completely different product architecture)

**These are structural advantages, not feature advantages.** Features can be copied. Architecture and pricing structure cannot.
